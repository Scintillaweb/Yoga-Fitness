When you find a practice you love, it is natural to want more of it. But more is not always better. Your body adapts and gets stronger while it recovers, which means rest days are not a break from your practice — they are part of it.

## Signs you might need more rest

Every body is different, but a few common signals suggest it is time to ease off:

- Poses that normally feel easy suddenly feel heavy.
- Soreness that lingers for several days.
- Restless sleep or low motivation to practice.
- Small aches that keep returning in the same place.

None of these are reasons to worry. They are simply information. Listening to them early helps you avoid bigger setbacks later.

## What a good rest day looks like

Rest does not have to mean doing nothing. Many people feel best with *active recovery* — gentle movement that keeps blood flowing without adding stress.

Some ideas:

1. A relaxed walk outdoors.
2. Ten minutes of slow stretching or a supported Yin pose or two.
3. A restorative class, where props hold you in comfortable shapes for several minutes.
4. A breathing practice before bed.

## Balancing strong and soft classes

If you practice several times a week, try mixing intensities. For example:

- **Two stronger sessions**, such as Vinyasa Flow or Power & Balance.
- **One or two softer sessions**, such as Yin & Restore or Breathwork Lab.
- **At least one full rest day.**

This rhythm lets you build strength and stamina while giving your joints and nervous system time to settle.

## Sleep: the overlooked recovery tool

Quality sleep supports almost every aspect of recovery. A short evening routine — dimming the lights, putting your phone away and a few minutes of slow breathing — can make it easier to wind down.

## Rest without guilt

It can be hard to rest when you are motivated. Try reframing it: a rest day is a deliberate choice that helps you keep practicing for years, not weeks.

If you are unsure how to balance your week, ask your coach. They can help you build a schedule that matches your goals, your energy and your life.
