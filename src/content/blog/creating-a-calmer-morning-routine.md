Many mornings start in a rush: an alarm, a phone full of notifications and a race to get out the door. It does not have to be that way. You do not need an hour-long ritual to start the day calmly — just a few intentional minutes.

## Delay the scroll

Try leaving your phone alone for the first fifteen minutes after you wake. Messages and news will still be there. Giving your mind a quiet start makes it easier to choose how you respond to the day, rather than reacting to it.

A practical tip: charge your phone outside the bedroom and use a simple alarm clock.

## Move for five minutes

A short sequence wakes up the body without feeling like a workout. Try this:

1. **Child's pose** — five slow breaths.
2. **Cat and cow** — eight rounds, moving with the breath.
3. **Downward-facing dog** — pedal the feet gently for five breaths.
4. **Standing forward fold** — let the head hang heavy.
5. **Roll up to standing** and reach the arms overhead.

That is it. Five minutes, no mat required if you have a soft rug.

## Pair it with something warm

A cup of tea, warm water with lemon or your usual coffee can become a small anchor for your routine. Drink it slowly, ideally without a screen in front of you. Notice the temperature, the smell and the first light of the day.

## Set one intention

Before you leave the house, choose a single word or phrase for the day. *Patience. Steady. Kind.* Return to it when things get busy. It is a small, portable reminder of how you want to feel.

> You cannot control everything that happens today, but you can choose how you begin it.

## Make it yours

The best morning routine is one you enjoy and can repeat. Some people prefer journaling, others a short walk or a few minutes of breathwork. Start with one element, keep it for a week and build from there.

## On busy mornings

Some days there is no time for any of it. On those mornings, take three slow breaths before you open the front door. Even a tiny pause counts.
